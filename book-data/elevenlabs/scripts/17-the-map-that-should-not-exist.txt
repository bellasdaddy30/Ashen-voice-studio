Zik: Nothing happened before.

Narrator: En-HEH-doo said.

Narrator: En-HEH-doo said.

Narrator: Then, before he could lift it—

Narrator: Across two facing sheets, wet ink spread from the binding.
Narrator: No pen touched it.
Narrator: Lines extended outward in thin black threads.
Narrator: Ninsun bent over the table.
Narrator: Her fingers hovered inches above the parchment.
Ninsun: This is not possible.
Zik: Welcome to my life,
Narrator: Zeek said.
Narrator: The map continued drawing itself.
Narrator: At first, the strokes formed the broad shape of Uruk.
Narrator: The river's bend.
Narrator: The east curtain wall.
Narrator: The temple district raised above the lower streets.
Narrator: Old roads disappeared beneath newer ones. Abandoned settlements clustered beyond the walls like broken teeth in the earth.
Narrator: Then the lines descended.
Narrator: Ink bled downward from the familiar geography, creating shafts, chambers, stairs, and inverted towers beneath the city. Some structures hung below others like roots from an overturned tree. Others occupied spaces that could not exist together, overlapping without crossing.
Narrator: Ninsun moved around the table.
Ninsun: It is not a surface plan.
Narrator: Zeek leaned closer.
Zik: Old surveyors liked details.
Narrator: She frowned.
Ninsun: No.
Narrator: Zeek looked up.
Zik: No?
Ninsun: I answered too quickly.

Ninsun: If this were a city plan…

Ninsun: …the temple should sit here.
Narrator: A second mark.
Ninsun: The river…
Narrator: Another line.
Ninsun: The gate…

Narrator: Her stylus hovered above the clay.
Ninsun: It doesn't work.

Enhedu: Perhaps whoever drew it was a poor surveyor.
Ninsun: No.
Narrator: Ninsun tapped the journal.
Ninsun: The measurements are too consistent.
Enhedu: So what's wrong?
Ninsun: We are.

Narrator: Then another ninety.
Narrator: Nothing changed.
Narrator: She looked at the drawing one last time.
Narrator: Her eyes widened slightly.
Ninsun: It isn't meant to be viewed from above.

Ninsun: I don't know.

Ninsun: A section requires a scale. This has none.
